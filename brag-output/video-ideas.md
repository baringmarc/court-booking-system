## Fix before posting the current poster

In `brag.jpg`, "Venues pay ₱0." sits next to "− Plotline fees −₱600", subtracted from the owner's revenue. A quick reader thinks the venue pays ₱600. That amount is the players' ₱10 fees passing through. Relabel the line (for example "Booking fees (paid by players)") or use a frame without the revenue split.

## Rules for every video

- **Payments:** say "GCash, Maya or bank". Online card payments (PayMongo) are off by default and the marketing site no longer mentions them.
- **Call to action:** "Put your venue on Plotline" or "Message us". There is no free trial and no self-serve signup, so never "Sign up free".
- **Fees:** come from `docs/facts.json`: venues pay ₱0 (no setup, no monthly fee); players add a ₱10 booking fee per confirmed online booking.
- **Data:** fictional names, references and amounts only, never production data.
- **Look:** the app's real UI, Fraunces and DM Sans, the Plotline palette (cream, espresso, sage, terracotta), as in the launch video.
- **Format:** 9:16 vertical for Reels, TikTok and FB unless noted. Captions on screen, since most people watch on silent.

## The ideas

### 0. Vertical cutdowns of the launch video
- **What:** three 6–10s 9:16 cuts from the existing video: the hook ("An empty court earns ₱0." with the grid filling), the one-tap approve, and "Venues pay ₱0." (with the poster fix above).
- **Why:** reuses `work/index.html`, nearly no new work.

### 1. Messenger chaos vs. Plotline
- **Hook:** "How many 'available pa po?' did you answer today?"
- **Shows:** split screen. Left: piles of Messenger chats, GCash screenshots, a paper logbook with 8 PM written twice. Right: players booking themselves on the slot grid.
- **Format:** 20–30s vertical, Taglish captions.

### 2. Post your open courts in 10 seconds (M31)
- **Hook:** "Still typing your open slots on Facebook every morning?"
- **Shows:** Post open slots on `/admin` → the 1080×1350 image in the venue's colors and logo → Download picture → Copy caption → pasted on the venue's FB page.
- **Format:** 15s vertical.
- **Why:** the newest feature, and it removes a daily chore.

### 3. Never double-booked
- **Hook:** "Two players. One 8 PM slot. Same second."
- **Shows:** two phones tap the same slot. One gets it, the other sees it's taken. Caption: "The database won't allow it."
- **Format:** 10s loop, works on silent autoplay.

### 4. The ₱0 math, explained
- **Hook:** "What's the catch?"
- **Shows:** player pays ₱400 + ₱10. The venue keeps ₱400, the ₱10 is Plotline's. No setup fee, no monthly fee.
- **Format:** 15–20s.
- **Why:** answers the first objection and clears up the poster confusion.

### 5. Fill your slow hours (promo rates, M19)
- **Hook:** "Your 2 PM is empty. Again."
- **Shows:** owner sets a Tue–Thu 2–5 PM promo on `/admin/schedule`, the promo label appears on the player's grid, the slots fill.
- **Format:** 15s.

### 6. Your regulars, on autopilot (M30)
- **Hook:** "Same players, same time, every week."
- **Shows:** the reminder email at 6 PM the day before, "Same time next week" on the booking page, the rebooking counted in earnings.
- **Format:** 20s.

### 7. It's your brand, not ours
- **Hook:** "Your courts. Your name. Your colors."
- **Shows:** theme presets swapping, the venue logo, `yourvenue.plotline.fit`, the link preview in Messenger, Add to Home Screen with the venue's own icon.
- **Format:** 15s.

### 8. A day at the front desk
- **Hook:** "Here's what running a venue looks like now."
- **Shows:** morning: post open slots. Noon: the "(2)" tab alert and one-tap approve. Night: the day view and earnings.
- **Format:** 45–60s mini-doc, for YouTube or a pinned FB post (16:9 and 9:16).

### 9. Booking shouldn't take 20 messages
- **Hook:** POV: "you just want to play at 7."
- **Shows:** 3 taps → payment QR → receipt upload → Confirmed → calendar invite → reminder email.
- **Format:** 15s.
- **Why:** made for venues to repost on their own pages, which brings players in.

### 10. Live stacks for open play (M29)
- **Hook:** "No more crowding around the whiteboard."
- **Shows:** the organizer runs the stack, players scan a QR, the board, Up next and standings update live on their phones, the share cards.
- **Format:** 20–30s.
- **Why:** pickleball groups share these widely, and organizers can use it at plotline.fit without signing in.

### 11. Venue spotlight
- **What:** 30–45s owner interview mixed with screen recordings of their own venue page, paired with a quote from the `reviews` table.
- **When:** once real venues are live and agree to appear.

### 12. We set it up with you
- **Hook:** "Is this hard to set up?"
- **Shows:** the invite email, the setup checklist, the owner manual PDF, then the venue live.
- **Why:** turns "no self-serve signup" into a selling point.
