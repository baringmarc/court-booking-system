## Does the marketing experience earn trust and turn interest into activation?

### Takeaway
Plotline already looks like a coherent product with a distinctive warm brand and a credible owner interface. The next gains are clarity, proof, and a shorter route to a useful demonstration and a qualified conversation. A wholesale redesign would be lower value than repairing promise/readiness gaps and measuring the funnel.

### Inferences
- **P0/P1, small effort:** audit every marketing promise against production capability before recruiting pilots; qualify/remove per-venue automatic payments until real onboarding is supported, distinguish request submitted/payment review/confirmed, and put optional processor fees beside pricing. Acceptance: each claim has a shipped-capability owner and a live proof or explicit planned label.
- **P1, small-to-medium:** move an owner demo preview near the hero; label secondary actions “Try player booking” and “Watch owner demo.” Keep current brand. Reduce duplicate fee explanations and let one pricing section carry the detailed terms. Mobile visitors should see a real product outcome within one scroll. Compare demo engagement and qualified lead conversion, not just pageviews.
- **P1, medium:** shorten first-contact intake to name, preferred contact method and venue; collect court count/location/setup details after interest, or use a two-step progressive form. This requires a shared API-contract change, not frontend-only deletion. Keep assisted onboarding; self-serve signup is not automatically better at this stage. Add a realistic response-time promise only if staffed, clear next step, and optionally a scheduling link. Measure form start→success, median first response, demo→pilot, lead→first real booking.
- **P1, small initially:** replace the empty review display with a truthful pilot invitation or useful assurance about guided setup. Secure 2–3 permissioned pilot stories with measured before/after manual admin effort, approval time, and repeat booking. Never fabricate logos, reviews or operational claims. A branded support mailbox would modestly improve continuity, but is secondary to reliable response.
- **P1, medium:** instrument a privacy-conscious funnel: marketing visit→player demo/owner demo→contact started→lead saved→call→venue configured→published→first confirmed real booking→week-four active venue. Use server-side lifecycle events where already available and minimal frontend events; never record proof images, contact data or guest access tokens in event payloads. Assess disclosure implications before introducing analytics; avoid claiming user conversion failures without data.
- **P2, medium:** prerender/public-page SEO, unique metadata/canonical URLs, sitemap, then a few genuinely useful Philippine venue-owner pages and real permissioned venue pages. Do not generate thin city pages before there is supply/content. Search Console/organic-qualified leads and actual indexed pages are better measures than raw article count.

## What does the live design reveal about continuity with the booking product?

### Takeaway
The brand is consistent across marketing and booking. The clearest observed design issue is functional hierarchy on the mobile booking page: selecting a slot is pushed below a large date selector and optional information.

### Cited Findings
- At 390×844, `/book` initially showed venue header, “Book a court”, a large date card, sport chips, and expanded court-photo cards; no availability grid was in the first screen. After collapsing “About the badminton courts”, the grid header only began at the bottom of the screen; date controls, explanatory copy, and a seven-state legend still preceded it. At 1280×900 with the gallery collapsed, several availability rows were visible. This was directly observed on the live demo, not inferred solely from CSS. [Demo booking](https://claudex-courts.plotline.fit/book)
- The marketing hero is a static drawn booking grid and button; its sample button says ₱800 without the booking fee, while surrounding copy explains the fee. The current drawn player walkthrough is an intentional prior choice, not a missing unimplemented screenshot feature. [PhoneMockup.tsx:38](/Users/macbookpro/Documents/Projects/plotline-site/src/components/PhoneMockup.tsx:38) [site.ts:116](/Users/macbookpro/Documents/Projects/plotline-site/src/config/site.ts:116) [TASKS.md:11](/Users/macbookpro/Documents/Projects/plotline-site/TASKS.md:11)

### Inferences
- **P1, medium:** compress date controls, default supplementary gallery closed on booking (keep photography prominent on venue home), reveal useful available times sooner, retain a visible booking summary after selection. Add “next available” or morning/afternoon/evening shortcuts only if user testing demonstrates a need. Acceptance: first useful slots visible without scrolling at 390×844, and no loss of keyboard/screen-reader operation. Measure time-to-first-slot and abandonment before slot selection.
- **P2, small:** keep drawings if desired, but visibly label illustration, align amounts/states with the real flow, and show one current real player screenshot or short clip elsewhere. A clean marketing mockup should not create a false expectation of immediate confirmation or a simpler payment flow.
- Prefer task-oriented visual hierarchy over more themes or decorative animation. No evidence gathered supports a full brand replacement.

### Gaps
- Not a comprehensive mobile/browser/accessibility audit. Light theme, small 360px viewport, landscape, iOS Safari and slower-network behavior need separate verification.
- Player researcher owns deeper checkout/cancellation/return-booking evidence; these observations complement it.

## What should Plotline learn from similar SaaS without copying their entire scope?

### Takeaway
Plotline's useful wedge is a simple, locally appropriate, venue-owned booking operation with hands-on setup. Low flat fees and guest booking are competitive features but are not unique. Defend the wedge through easier daily work, trustworthy payment states and demonstrable local service before taking on a marketplace or enterprise club suite.

### Cited Findings
- **Taradula (closest Philippine commercial comparator):** official owner page advertises free setup/monthly service, direct owner GCash, ₱15 per online booking paid by player, weekly fee invoices, free walk-ins, recurring bookings, player history, restricted staff access, and store credit. It also advertises discovery by place/time and city pages. These are vendor claims, not features tested hands-on. [Taradula owner offer](https://taradula.com/list-your-venue)
- **Sparrk (Philippine discovery/open-play comparator):** official site combines court discovery by city/sport/time, open-play participation, club-event payments, roster/queue tools, and a venue calendar incorporating walk-ins. Its marketing claims and listings demonstrate a broader marketplace/community strategy, not proof that Plotline must build all of it. [Sparrk](https://www.sparrk.ph/)
- **CourtReserve (mature club operations):** official site covers reservations, events, memberships, lessons, leagues, POS, billing and reporting. Its official public-booking guide explicitly supports a public URL and guest reservations without a CourtReserve account, with exceptions such as waitlists/team events requiring sign-in. Do not claim Plotline uniquely eliminates mandatory accounts. [CourtReserve](https://courtreserve.com/) [Public-booking guide](https://help.courtreserve.com/en/articles/11787240-public-booking-setup-guide-and-overview)
- **Playtomic (group-payment complexity):** current official help explains split payments for private app bookings, sport restrictions, guarantee mechanisms and liability for outstanding shares; website private bookings use single payment. This is useful evidence of the operational complexity behind an appealing “split payment” feature. [Playtomic split/single payments](https://helpmanager.playtomic.com/hc/en-gb/articles/20535331224721-Split-Payment-vs-Single-payment)
- **AllBooked by Skedda (venue-rule comparator):** official site describes recurring bookings, quotas, cancellation controls, check-in prompts, analytics and integrations. Useful benchmark for eventual utilization reporting and rules, not a requirement for Plotline's first pilot. [AllBooked](https://www.allbooked.com/)

### Inferences
- Stop treating a ₱5 advantage over an adjacent published fee as the main differentiator. Prioritize concrete outcomes: less Messenger coordination, quick staff approval, reliable courts-and-hours availability, accountable refunds, and migration/setup assistance. Do not change price without measured support/infra/collections costs.
- At the present ₱10 fee, 100 confirmed billable transactions yield ₱1,000 gross platform fees and 1,000 yield ₱10,000, before expenses and collections losses. This is arithmetic from shared facts, not profit or a demand forecast. Track contribution per active venue and support minutes per 100 bookings before promising unlimited service forever. [facts.json](/Users/macbookpro/Documents/Projects/court-booking-system/docs/facts.json)
- Highest-value competitive capabilities to validate with pilot owners: fast staff-created/walk-in reservations, returning-player history, recurring group booking, check-in/no-show markers, payment/refund reconciliation, reminders and notifications, and utilization by court/day/hour. Some may already be implemented or feature-flagged: defer exact status to owner/player research; do not label all missing.
- Open-play seat registration/waitlist is a coherent future expansion if pilot venues run paid open play; first validate attendance and fee responsibilities. A marketplace adds a second acquisition problem (players as well as venues), so prove density in one locality before cross-venue search. Native apps, AI booking assistants, full POS, complex memberships and split payments can wait until demand makes their maintenance worthwhile.
- Track competitor breadth for sales objections, not as a blanket roadmap. Plotline should be easier for a small Philippine venue to operate, not a smaller copy of every competitor.

### Gaps
- Vendor claims were not independently verified by transactions/accounts. No competitive benchmark of actual booking speed, reliability, customer satisfaction or market share was conducted.
- Playtomic main pricing pages returned HTTP 403 to web fetch; no current pricing comparison is asserted. Taradula published ₱15 is verified as page copy at review time, not a contractual quote.
- No recommendation here changes accepted business facts, product code or planned tasks; all are proposals for prioritization.
