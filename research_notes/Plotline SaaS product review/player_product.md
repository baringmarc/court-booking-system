# Player booking product and UX

## What works today and what was actually inspected

### Takeaway
Plotline already has a credible guest-first booking engine and a recognizable, warm visual identity. The biggest next opportunity is to make the transaction feel unmistakably safe and complete at every step, then make returning to play easier; adding a large feature catalog should come after those improvements.

### Cited Findings

No fresh app runtime session or production payment was performed; no automated tests were run. ### Inferences
- The app's value proposition is well suited to an owner who wants fewer booking messages and one reliable schedule while retaining familiar manual payments. It does not need an entirely new visual brand.
- Current design spends a lot of screen space explaining and displaying information; the strongest redesign would prioritize the next action based on booking state and move supporting information behind concise disclosure.

### Gaps
- No real-player usability recordings, funnel data, payment-review latency, no-show baseline, or repeat-booking rate were available in this sub-audit. Proposed conversion improvements are hypotheses to measure, not claimed percentage lifts.
- Production deployment status was not independently verified. Another researcher covers current public visuals.

## What should improve in the core journey first

### Takeaway
Fix payment clarity, booking-state language, cancellation disclosure, and recovery before investing heavily in expansion features. These improvements target the moment when the player is committing money and the venue is making an operational promise.

### Cited Findings
1. **P1 — Show the full payable amount before Continue.** The selection summary sums only court-slot prices, and its prominent number is unlabeled as a court-only subtotal. Checkout adds the booking fee. Example at the documented ₱10 fee: a two-hour ₱800 selection becomes ₱810 at checkout. This is a real source-level difference, not an arithmetic bug. Retain hourly court prices in the grid, but show “Court ₱800 + booking fee ₱10 = total ₱810” in the selection bar, with the same fee source as checkout. Effort: small–medium. Acceptance: selected, checkout and created-booking totals reconcile under normal/promo pricing and a fee change; measure selection-to-checkout abandonment. [Summary calculation](/Users/macbookpro/Documents/Projects/court-booking-system/src/features/booking/selection.ts:75) [Displayed number](/Users/macbookpro/Documents/Projects/court-booking-system/src/features/booking/SelectionBar.tsx:27) [Checkout fee](/Users/macbookpro/Documents/Projects/court-booking-system/src/features/booking/CheckoutPage.tsx:123)

2. **P1 — Make “reserved,” “paid,” and “confirmed” unmistakable; put the next action first.** Checkout says “Confirm booking” although it reserves an unpaid hold. The next page correctly says “Booking reserved,” but puts a large Save this link card before the booking summary and payment. Rename the CTA to “Reserve and continue to payment,” use a compact step/status summary, and place payment amount, timer and payment action near the top. Keep link-saving accessible and prominent enough to prevent loss, but compact it and report email-send status if known. Once staff approve, lead with “You're confirmed” plus when/where/arrival instructions. Effort: small–medium. Acceptance: five target users can explain whether they have paid and whether they may play without prompting; measure reserve-to-proof completion. [CTA](/Users/macbookpro/Documents/Projects/court-booking-system/src/features/booking/CheckoutForm.tsx:84) [Page hierarchy](/Users/macbookpro/Documents/Projects/court-booking-system/src/features/booking/BookingPage.tsx:54) [Save link](/Users/macbookpro/Documents/Projects/court-booking-system/src/features/booking/BookingPage.tsx:167) [Payment heading](/Users/macbookpro/Documents/Projects/court-booking-system/src/features/booking/BookingSummary.tsx:75)

3. **P1 — Structure and show the cancellation/refund policy before commitment.** Checkout only links to a freeform info section if its title matches `/cancel/i`; no matching title means no link. The player learns their exact cancellation deadline on the booking page; paid cancellation says to contact the venue about a refund. Add explicit structured settings for cancellation cutoff, refund eligibility/fee treatment and contact method; show a short summary beside the reserve CTA and a stored policy snapshot on the booking. This is a product recommendation, not a legal-compliance finding. Effort: medium. Acceptance: every payable booking shows a clear cutoff and refund treatment before reserve; a change to venue policy cannot silently rewrite the accepted policy. [Optional title matching](/Users/macbookpro/Documents/Projects/court-booking-system/src/features/venue/VenueInfo.tsx:144) [Cancellation page](/Users/macbookpro/Documents/Projects/court-booking-system/src/features/booking/CancelBooking.tsx:19) [Paid cancellation warning](/Users/macbookpro/Documents/Projects/court-booking-system/src/features/booking/CancelBooking.tsx:44)

4. **P1 — Give manual-payment review an operational promise and escalation route.** After upload, the current message is only “We're checking your payment. This page updates when staff confirm it.” There is no review-time estimate or inline contact action there. Add a realistic venue-configured “normally checked within…” expectation, receipt timestamp, next notification channel and “contact venue” action for an overdue review. Pair with staff overdue-proof alerts; do not promise automatic confirmation before it exists. Effort: medium across UI and operations. Acceptance: pending proof always shows what happens next; measure median/p95 review time and number of proofs still unreviewed near session start. [Pending message](/Users/macbookpro/Documents/Projects/court-booking-system/src/features/booking/BookingSummary.tsx:101) [Thirty-second review polling](/Users/macbookpro/Documents/Projects/court-booking-system/src/features/booking/hooks.ts:77)

5. **P1/P2 — Make failed/expired paths recoverable without starting over.** An expired hold says “You can book again” but supplies no rebooking button in that state. Missing guest access sends people toward signup/login; the create hook requests guest email fire-and-forget and discards errors. Add safe “find another time” or “try this slot again” actions with fresh availability, and a privacy-preserving resend/recovery flow for the booking link (rate-limited and non-enumerating). Do not rebook automatically or duplicate a payment. Effort: medium; email recovery has security considerations. Acceptance: expired or lost-link users have a direct usable next step; track support contacts for lost links and abandoned expired holds. [Expired state](/Users/macbookpro/Documents/Projects/court-booking-system/src/features/booking/BookingSummary.tsx:113) [Missing link](/Users/macbookpro/Documents/Projects/court-booking-system/src/features/booking/BookingPage.tsx:58) [Fire-and-forget link request](/Users/macbookpro/Documents/Projects/court-booking-system/src/features/booking/hooks.ts:44)

6. **P2 — Let the player find a suitable free time faster on phones.** The booking screen renders a detailed court gallery open by default above the slot grid. The grid is a scrollable time-by-court table with a 70vh cap, so busy venues can require scrolling in multiple directions. Keep the grid for visual planners, but default the court details to compact summaries on returning visits, offer “earliest available,” duration and morning/afternoon/evening filters, and consider an available-times list for narrow screens. Avoid a cosmetic wholesale redesign. Effort: medium. Acceptance: benchmark 360px users finding a two-hour evening slot in a 6+ court venue, with keyboard/screen-reader checks; measure time to first valid selection. [Court gallery ordering](/Users/macbookpro/Documents/Projects/court-booking-system/src/features/booking/BookPage.tsx:119) [Scroll container](/Users/macbookpro/Documents/Projects/court-booking-system/src/index.css:435) [Slot table](/Users/macbookpro/Documents/Projects/court-booking-system/src/features/booking/SlotGrid.tsx:68)

7. **P2 — Make manual payment a single guided method flow.** The app renders all payment-account cards, then a separate “Paid with” dropdown, amount, reference and proof. Show/select a payment method once, reveal its account/QR and preselect it for proof submission; keep amount editable for genuine mismatch reporting. The current QR/download and copy helpers should remain. Effort: medium. Acceptance: selected payee/method remains consistent through receipt upload, users can change it explicitly, and high-value accidental wrong-payee cases are tested. Measure upload abandonment/errors. [All accounts](/Users/macbookpro/Documents/Projects/court-booking-system/src/features/booking/PaymentAccountList.tsx:13) [Independent method selection](/Users/macbookpro/Documents/Projects/court-booking-system/src/features/booking/ProofForm.tsx:65) [Payment layout](/Users/macbookpro/Documents/Projects/court-booking-system/src/features/booking/BookingSummary.tsx:89)

### Inferences
- These are mostly conversion/trust improvements to implemented behavior; none proves the system is broken. Priority P1 means before broader commercial onboarding, not necessarily a critical security defect.
- The paid-cancellation gap can first be improved with transparent manual refund tracking and a named next step; a full automated refund integration is not required to improve the player experience.
- A public booking grid currently displays first names alongside reserved times. This is a deliberate existing decision, but should be revisited as a privacy/product choice: default public labels to “Booked” and offer clearly explained opt-in if the community benefit is real. Do not claim a legal violation. [Public first names](/Users/macbookpro/Documents/Projects/court-booking-system/src/features/booking/SlotGrid.tsx:102)

### Gaps
- No estimate is available for how much each friction point costs in bookings. Test with actual Philippine players using GCash/Maya, including iPhone receipt upload and weak connections, before accepting a full redesign.
- Current homepage “from” price is based on default hourly court rates, not a minimum over actual dated rates/promos; whether this creates misleading expectations depends on venue configuration. Consider labeling it “standard rate from…” or deriving a precise current offer. [Home pricing](/Users/macbookpro/Documents/Projects/court-booking-system/src/routes/HomePage.tsx:77)

## Which expansion features would create the most repeat value

### Takeaway
The next features should close the loop from booking to attendance to another booking. A sports marketplace, memberships and native apps should follow demonstrated demand rather than become prerequisites for the first successful venues.

### Cited Findings
8. **P2 — Add attendance and repeat-booking tools: calendar, reminders, book again.** My bookings separates upcoming/past and links to individual records; it has no rebook control. Repository searches of source and Edge Functions found lifecycle emails but no scheduled pre-session reminder, `.ics`/calendar export, waitlist or recurring-booking implementation. SPEC explicitly excludes recurring bookings, memberships, native apps and SMS. Start with calendar export, one email reminder and “Book this court again” that rechecks price/availability. Effort: medium. Acceptance: reminders cancel/change correctly after cancellation/reschedule; calendar links never expose private guest management tokens; rebook requires user confirmation. Metrics: no-show rate (requires attendance capture), 30-day repeat booking, reminder delivery. [My bookings](/Users/macbookpro/Documents/Projects/court-booking-system/src/features/account/MyBookingsPage.tsx:55) [Lifecycle email kinds](/Users/macbookpro/Documents/Projects/court-booking-system/supabase/functions/_shared/emailTemplates.ts:5) [Non-goals](/Users/macbookpro/Documents/Projects/court-booking-system/SPEC.md:17)

9. **P2/P3 — Turn open play from a notice into an optional registration product.** Current sessions provide title/time/courts, details and external links; the dialog explicitly says it cannot be booked online and tells users to join via links. That can be appropriate for a simple pilot, but owners still need separate tools to count players. An incremental feature would add capacity, attendee registration, level tags, organizer approval/payment rules and cancellation, then a waitlist that offers a released place for a limited time. Keep private court rentals separate from per-person session bookings. Effort: large; a new lifecycle and billing decision, not a small button. Acceptance: no over-capacity joins, clear waitlist ordering and timeout, exact attendee/payment state, session cancellation communications. Metric: external-message volume per session and fill rate. [Open play dialog](/Users/macbookpro/Documents/Projects/court-booking-system/src/features/openPlay/OpenPlayDialog.tsx:53)

10. **P2/P3 — Add a player reschedule request before broad self-service moving.** Staff rescheduling already exists, including split continuations and price preservation. Players can cancel but cannot directly request a move in the booking page. Start with “Request a different time,” with venue decision and clear original booking protection; later expose self-service moves within explicit rules about cutoff, duration and price difference. Effort: medium for a request workflow; large for automatic repricing/refunds. Acceptance: original slot remains until an approved atomic move, no double reservation, one clear notification, price difference shown before commitment. Measure cancellations that could have been saved and staff messages per move. [Staff reschedule](/Users/macbookpro/Documents/Projects/court-booking-system/src/features/admin/RescheduleSection.tsx:18) [Player cancel section](/Users/macbookpro/Documents/Projects/court-booking-system/src/features/booking/BookingPage.tsx:141) [Move rules](/Users/macbookpro/Documents/Projects/court-booking-system/SPEC.md:151)

### Inferences
- Suggested sequence: pricing/state/policy clarity and proof-review operations → lightweight recovery and reminders/rebook → pilot-driven open-play registration and rescheduling → recurring booking, memberships or marketplace only when owners repeatedly request them.
- Add explicit product funnel events (availability viewed, selection made, checkout opened, reservation created, proof submitted, confirmed, cancelled, repeat booked) with no names/emails/tokens in payloads. No product-analytics integration was found in the inspected source searches; Sentry initialization exists for errors, which does not establish a measured conversion funnel. [Sentry boot](/Users/macbookpro/Documents/Projects/court-booking-system/src/main.tsx:22)
- Validate with five players and two owners doing real representative tasks. Collect completion rate, time, observed confusion and assistance required. Those numbers would turn design opinions into ranked product work.

### Gaps
- Cross-venue login and discovery are explicitly later in the SaaS plan, but the current value of building them depends on how many real venues/players are active and overlapping. Do not let a marketplace distract from making the first venue reliable. [Later scope](/Users/macbookpro/Documents/Projects/court-booking-system/docs/SAAS-PLAN.md)
- This sub-audit does not establish a defect in automatic refunds or live PayMongo because those are current non-goals/planned expansion, not implemented promises. The other research streams should check marketing claims against these boundaries.
